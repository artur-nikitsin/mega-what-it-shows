The problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

A tenant in a day-first locale writes 7/4 for 7 April. The shared parser reads it as 4 July. The day and the month swap, so the booking, the invite and the reminder land on the wrong date. This is the bug #4471 names. It reaches the desk through every channel: app, email and form.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first. The form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first, so the date flips. Only tenants in a day-first locale that run booking-v3 get it, and only for dates typed into the form. The parser fix does not touch it: here the form’s hint and the form’s reader disagree with each other.

one-day: the date moves by one day

These reports are not a swap. A Tuesday shows as a Monday. A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking was made. The desk read these as one-day moves (desk notes), and the cause table on #4471 files them under parser by tenant. A parser fix does not fix them, so they get their own ticket here as well. They point to a time zone bug: a date sent as midnight in one zone and shown in another. Engineering must confirm the cause.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges reports on the subject line. A subject does not say which bug a report is about. The same subject comes from different problems, and different subjects come from the same problem. #4471 holds many subjects, all merged as one. Try it.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser: an en-US browser gets hint. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form. The table shows how far the tags are from the causes.
  • The cause table on #4471 already showed a second cause and a note about one-day moves. The page text above it still said “one issue”.

Decision 0001 is accepted, so this page does not edit it. To change how the desk merges, write a new decision record that replaces it.
